# Review of areas 6, 7, 8, 9 and 12

Quick runs (13 Sonnet reviewers, read-only) on the branch `docs/combined-review-areas-3-4-5-10-11` (on top of `feat/publication-date`). 146 raw findings: homepage curation 36, taxonomy and tags 38, profiles and credits 29, site information and audit log 19, design system sweep 24. No Critical findings. Anonymous exposure of account-less credits is closed (every public projection hides their slug). Site information writes are administrator-only at both layers and stored links are limited to official https hosts. The audit table cannot be updated or deleted by any application role. Full per-reviewer reports were saved in the reviewer's checkout (`docs/code-reviews/raw/area{6,7,8,9,12}-*.md`, not committed); the area 8 privacy reviewer had no write tool, so its findings are recorded here only. IDs `D-nn` are new to this report; source IDs read `area/reviewer-number`. Status uses the same labels as [COMBINED-REVIEW-REPORT.md](COMBINED-REVIEW-REPORT.md): **Fix now**, **Cheap**, **Plan**, **Deferred**. Fix plan additions are in [COMBINED-FIX-PLAN.md](COMBINED-FIX-PLAN.md) (section "Additions from areas 6 to 9 and 12").

One refutation: the area 3 review flagged a probable deadlock between tag merge and article save. The area 7 review traced the lock order (article row, then draft tags in id order, then `article_tags`) and found the main cycle cannot form. Only a narrow case remains (D-12).
